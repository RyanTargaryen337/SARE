## Vision

### 10x Check
The 10x version of Sàrè is the place a Nigerian food vendor checks every morning to know their cash position: every order from every channel (their own WhatsApp regulars now, Sàrè's marketplace later) settles to their bank automatically, with a daily message proving it, and with their own numbers showing how much sooner they are paid than on incumbent platforms. Delivery and discovery are layered on top of that trust, zone by zone. Effort over the base plan: about 1.2x (three small Phase 1 additions and one medium); value: the payout promise becomes visible and measured from the first week.
